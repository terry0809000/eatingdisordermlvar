# Research questions

1. Among observations where exercise was endorsed, what are the contemporaneous associations among eating-disorder behaviours, affect, body satisfaction, and fear of weight gain?

2. Does adding maladaptive exercise (`mal_ex`) alter the structure or centrality profile of the exercise-restricted contemporaneous network?

3. Which lagged affective, cognitive, or behavioural variables predict subsequent nodes within the same day?

4. Is maladaptive exercise more strongly connected contemporaneously with negative affect, compensatory behaviour, or binge eating, or does it appear primarily as a temporally predicted outcome?

5. How do shared-node strength estimates change between the base model and the additive model that includes maladaptive exercise?
